# CP05 controlled geographic reconciliation

**Status: partial, not approved for gameplay.** `CP05_GEO_REGISTRY.json` is a versioned reconciliation ledger, not a completed canonical registry or a map adapter. It retains all 1,174 configured question occurrences and eight India typing bonuses. No SVG or gameplay behavior was changed in this pass. The proposed world pilot remains gated by the unresolved geometry and policy cases below.

## Counts and scope

The ledger contains **989 reconciliation records**, not a proven count of unique geographic entities: 196 ISO-code-based country records, 36 India source-pair records, and 757 other subdivision/special-region question records. The latter remain separate because identical or similar labels alone do not prove identity. Therefore an exact total of unique geographic entities is **not yet established**. Of the country records, 189 occur in more than one game scope. The 196 include the Democratic Republic of Congo and Côte d'Ivoire, which appear in Africa but not in the configured world list.

## Evidence and safeguards

`tools/build-geo-registry.py` checks the SHA-256 of the existing 5.1.1 Natural Earth source archives and the pinned geoBoundaries India sample against `CP05_GEO_FEASIBILITY.json`. Country records associate a game-side country code from a flag, legacy SVG, or a documented matching world-code fallback with the unique corresponding Natural Earth 50m Admin-0 source code and feature ID. `ISO_A2` is preferred over `ISO_A2_EH`, so Australia's main feature is not confused with dependencies sharing the latter code. For technically reconciled countries the 50m feature is present and GEOS-valid, and the same `NE_ID` in 10m is present and GEOS-valid, with longitude/latitude area intersection-over-union at least 0.5. This threshold is an investigation heuristic, not an authoritative geometry standard, and neither shared-provider cross-scale agreement nor a legacy identifier proves an independently surveyed boundary. The exact features, code signals, hashes, cross-scale metric and source license are recorded per entity. Observed scope labels are not automatically promoted to approved aliases.

For 36 India records, Natural Earth Admin-1 and geoBoundaries gbOpen assign matching reported subdivision codes to the corresponding candidates. The record identifies both source features and licenses; their year discrepancy and classification/boundary limitations mean these associations remain unapproved. The `source-pair:india:*` identifier is provisional and does not assert that the code is current ISO. Natural Earth's Himachal Pradesh type attribute remains inconsistent with the game's state classification.

For the remaining 757 subdivision records, 700 have exactly one Natural Earth name candidate, 54 have none, and three have multiple candidates. Existing SVG IDs corroborate exactly one candidate code for 611 records, including Washington state (`US-WA` rather than `US-DC`), Buenos Aires province (`AR-B` rather than `AR-C`) and México state (`MX-MEX` rather than `MX-DIF`). The old SVG identity and rights are unverified, so this corroboration is evidence for further review, not independent administrative validation or a gameplay approval. France and Italy still require verified region data or verified aggregation of the appropriate departments/provinces.

`node tools/validate-geo-registry.cjs` checks occurrence preservation, duplicate records, identifier agreement, source-feature collisions, administrative level and present/valid geometry for reconciled and verified records. Tests exercise rejection of duplicate IDs, wrong levels, conflicting identifiers and invalid geometry. This validator checks ledger structure and stated evidence; it cannot establish the truth of that evidence or detect all bad boundaries. The reproducible generator requires `pyshp 3.1.6`, `shapely 2.2.0`, Node and local checksum-matching files described in `CP05_GEOGRAPHIC_RECOVERY.md`; run `PYTHONPATH=<temporary-package-directory> python3 tools/build-geo-registry.py <source-directory>`. The downloaded source data remain outside the project.

## Remaining cases and pilot decision

- **Seychelles, Maldives, Kiribati, Marshall Islands, Tuvalu, Monaco and Vatican City:** 50m and 10m same-ID polygons have low or zero cross-scale area overlap under the heuristic. Candidate identities are recorded, but the geometry discrepancy and very small target size require targeted inspection and a selection mechanism that retains all legitimate pieces. A different scale, verified source or accessible selection alternative are possible; no polygons should be fabricated. A world pilot using only the currently reconciled features would leave these seven questions unsupported.
- **Boundary policy:** 22 country records are on a conservative, non-exhaustive watchlist for disputed or sensitive boundaries. Natural Earth states its default is de facto and provides alternate points of view. A product-wide boundary/territory policy must be recorded and applied; choosing a source identifier alone does not resolve this editorial question. Until then a visually authoritative replacement risks presenting an unapproved boundary. The watchlist is not a definitive catalogue of disputes.
- **India:** the geoBoundaries metadata reports represented year 2011, while the inspected features name later reorganized entities. Trace the original revisions, verify classifications and boundaries, and settle the applicable disputed-boundary policy. The current eight union territories remain typing bonuses, not newly added locate questions.
- **Other subdivisions:** obtain an independently licensed administrative source and verify code, parent, level, effective date and geometry for each scope. For France and Italy, inspect verified regional sources or aggregate verified lower-level pieces. The 611 legacy-code corroborations are a prioritized queue, not a substitute for validation. Until this is done, replacing those editions risks misidentifying a target or losing coverage.

No world-map pilot was integrated: zero records are classified verified, seven world geometries need investigation, and boundary/territory policy and fair small-target interaction remain unresolved. Existing legacy SVG assets and their unresolved licensing remain in use. I-12, meaningful I-15 guard-removal regression evidence, accessible geographic selection, viewport controls, touch, cleanup and CP11 physical-device checks remain open.

Targeted source inspection (`tools/inspect-small-country-geometry.py`) makes the small-country concern concrete. At 1:50m the Maldives feature has two polygon parts; at 1:10m it has 176. Seychelles has one versus 26; Marshall Islands five versus 22; and Tuvalu one versus nine. The Vatican 50m polygon bounds are `(12.4275, 41.8976, 12.4392, 41.9062)`, while its 10m bounds are `(12.4527, 41.9028, 12.4540, 41.9039)`: the features do not overlap. These comparisons establish representation differences, not which source is authoritative or that every polygon is playable. They make a whole-world 50m substitution materially risky; a reviewed 10m or mixed-resolution source and accessible small-target interaction are candidates for further work.

## Full-world continuation (2026-11-01)

`CP05_WORLD_COVERAGE.json` supplements rather than replaces this registry's original reconciliation decisions. Of 194 questions, 193 source alpha-3 identifiers are corroborated against the current [UN M49 table](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/methodology/m49/); Taiwan's separate `TWN` identifier is evidenced by historical UN statistical material and the UN Population Division, while the current M49 table groups Taiwan under China. These statistical references do not settle sovereignty, boundaries or the permissibility of a map view. Eight identities had independent pilot evidence; the other 186 are code-corroborated, not independently approved. Original registry status counts remain 165 reconciled, 22 policy-dependent and seven ambiguous; the new evidence does not silently promote them.

The user's provisional de facto development baseline is recorded in the coverage report. Disputed entities remain explicitly flagged, and territorial control must not be presented as an accepted sovereignty claim. Natural Earth's default representation does not automatically approve any disputed identity. Preserve canonical identities and scoring across any future alternative geographic views, including India's. Release requires verification of relevant Indian mapping and distribution requirements.

## Machine-readable readiness (2026-11-01)

`CP05_READINESS.json` is a deterministic, conservative derivative of the registry, coverage, requirements, feasibility, and candidate manifest. It keeps the existing ledger disposition visible while assigning no gameplay approval: the 194 world targets are **0 VERIFIED, 165 AUTOMATED_ONLY, 7 AMBIGUOUS, 22 POLICY_DEPENDENT, and 0 BLOCKED**. Egypt's original checksum-recorded Natural Earth geometry remains invalid; a reproducible GEOS `make_valid` diagnostic repair produces eight valid parts, preserves bounds and representative-point containment, has a relative planar-area difference below `1e-12`, and has no positive-area overlap with Israel, Libya, Sudan, or any other target. That evidence removes a technical topology block but does not independently approve Egypt's boundary or promote it beyond policy-dependent. The report also retains all 36 India state/bonus records as policy-dependent, with the four decisions on effective date, Jammu and Kashmir/Ladakh presentation, attribution/provenance, and the 28-state/8-Union-Territory scope split. It reports the 54 no-candidate and three multi-candidate subnational discovery results. This artifact is a release gate, not a source of geographic truth or a change to the development-only candidate integration.
