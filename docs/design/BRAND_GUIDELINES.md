# Brand guidelines

MapMaster is a curious, capable guide: adventurous without being childish, clear without feeling institutional. The original compass-globe mark combines direction and global exploration. `assets/brand/icon.svg` is the compact app icon/favicon; `logo.svg` is a light-surface lockup, `logo-light.svg` a dark-surface lockup, and `icon-mono.svg` a monochrome treatment. Preserve the mark's proportions and space around it; use the icon alone in compact contexts. SVG is the sole icon format; raster platform icons and social previews require future export and validation.

System fonts fall back to platform sans and monospace. No paid fonts or icon packages were added. New logo SVGs are original project artwork; existing map and flag provenance remains subject to `docs/audit/ASSET_PROVENANCE.md`. Theme semantics are in `tokens.css`; avoid using color alone for answer state.
