Independent naming material supports Tokyo as the present conventional capital and Japanese as the official language, but the candidate must not silently flatten the historic/current capital claims or select an area value. The frozen record also retains Bonn as a capital claim, historical currency claims, and differing area claims. The evidence supports a current fact only; it does not authorise deletion or automatic choice among the retained temporal/area claims. Evidence: raw Q183 statements/qualifiers; [German Federal Government country profile](https://www.bundesregierung.de/breg-en/federal-government/germany-820098); [Federal Government chronology of the D-mark transition](https://www.bundesregierung.de/breg-de/schwerpunkte/deutsche-einheit/d-mark-wird-zahlungsmittel-353940).

Evidence: raw Q20 statements/qualifiers; [Norwegian Government: Svalbard status](https://www.regjeringen.no/no/dokumenter/prop2.-1-s-20252026/id3123279/?ch=1); [Norwegian Government: polar territories](https://www.regjeringen.no/en/documents/meld.-st.-35-20232024/id3054780/?ch=3).

## Founder-policy decisions carried forward or newly implicated

1. **Current versus historical claims:** a policy is still needed for date-qualified capital, currency, and area claims (Japan, Germany, Malta; previously Nigeria and Brazil). Raw claims must remain intact whichever presentation rule is approved.
2. **Territorial scope and continental framing:** decide which legal components and territorial conventions may be exposed in country facts (Norway; previously Morocco). No area/continent value has been selected here.
3. **Split capital/entity language:** retain the Batch 1 decision on conventional capital questions and Vatican City/Holy See definitions. This batch’s South Africa item is factually clear but does not settle the wider product terminology.
4. **Territorial-administration wording:** decide whether candidates saying a territory is “controlled by” a country are categorically excluded or must be rewritten only after explicit review (Andaman and Nicobar Islands). The raw OpenTDB wording cannot be silently altered.
