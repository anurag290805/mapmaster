## Content-quality and release blockers

City questions contain one correct country answer, four unique options, an ISO country-code mapping reference, and retain coordinates only as evidence metadata. Feature cards are not scored questions and do not claim that a generic GeoNames class uniquely identifies a feature. Alternative names, source-name mismatches, and territorial caveats stay visible in their individual v2 records.

Wikidata structured data remains CC0 with traceable provenance; GeoNames requires CC BY 4.0 credit, license link, and adaptation/modification indication before any release. Remaining actions are: explicit founder decision for each v2 item (or a documented reviewed bulk decision), production attribution placement for GeoNames, normal CP04/CP05/CP10 release checks, and independent legal review before any OpenTDB use.
