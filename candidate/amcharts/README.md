# CP05 amCharts source snapshots

This directory preserves the exact high-detail SVG files retrieved on 2026-11-05 from the amCharts pages recorded in `manifest.json`. The source files are intentionally unmodified: their SHA-256 values, path IDs and `data-name` fields are the reviewed evidence.

amCharts states on each recorded source page that these maps are available under CC BY 4.0 for commercial use when the credit is retained. Any future display of a derived or copied candidate must visibly provide the manifest's `Map by amCharts` credit linking to `https://www.amcharts.com/svg-maps/`, and must retain the other CC BY 4.0 requirements.

These snapshots are not in `svg/`, are not bundled or precached by Vite, and cannot be selected by the live game. The mappings establish source-path association only—not geographic approval, boundary policy, touch/keyboard reachability, scoring integration, or public-beta clearance. In particular, China `CN-TW` and UAE `OM-MU`, `AE-NZ1`, `AE-NZ2` are explicit noninteractive source context, not game answers or policy decisions.
