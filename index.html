FIELD GUIDE / HOW TO PLAY

A little help
goes a long way.

Choose a game and a region, then pick practice or a timed challenge.

Choose your game

Type names from memory, locate a named place on the map, identify a flag, or name a capital. Flags are available for world and continent scopes where flag data exists.

Practice & challenge

Practice has no countdown. Challenge uses a timer; locate also asks for lives. Known issue: an incorrect flag or capital answer in challenge can end the game unexpectedly.

Scoring, hints & results

Correct answers add to the score and streak. Hints reveal a clue; in timed play they can subtract time. Results appear at the end. Typing currently accepts some partial or repeated answers incorrectly.

Maps, keyboard & touch

Use the map to locate places; wheel or pinch to zoom. Map selection currently requires pointing and some targets or map files do not work. North and South America maps fail to load; a keyboard map alternative is planned.

Guest preferences

Theme preference is stored on this device when storage is available. Sound is kept for this visit. Game history and achievements are not yet saved.
